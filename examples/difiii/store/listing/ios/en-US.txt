===== Description (1391/4000) =====
Spent money? Log it before you forget.

Difiii is an expense tracker built for speed. Type "Lunch 6000" and it's saved — amount, title and date in one go. No sign-up, no bank to connect, no internet needed.

WHY DIFIII
• Fast: log an expense in under 10 seconds.
• Offline: everything works without a connection.
• No account: open the app and start. No email, no password.
• Private: your records stay on your phone. We don't collect them, sell them or show you ads.

CAPTURE
• Type it the way you'd say it: "Taxi 2500", "Groceries 18 000".
• Attach a voice note to remember the details.
• Snap a receipt photo and keep it with the expense.
• Edit or delete with a swipe.

BUDGETS
• Set a budget for the week, the month or a trip.
• Track every expense by category: food, transport, bills and more.
• Plan upcoming expenses and expected income, and see your projected balance.
• Run several budgets at the same time.

INSIGHTS
• See where your money went by category.
• Compare months with a 6-month trend.
• Check daily totals in your records.

MADE FOR YOUR MONEY
• 20 African currencies, including CFA franc (XAF, XOF), naira, cedi, shilling, rand and dirham, plus USD, EUR, GBP and more.
• English and French.
• Light and dark themes.
• App lock with your fingerprint or face.

Your data lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Privacy policy: https://difiii.com/privacy
